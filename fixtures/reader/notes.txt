A plain text file, opened as source.

This fixture used to say the opposite: `kinas open notes.txt` exited 65, because the
reader read only .md and .mdx. It is the file the widening is measured against, so its
text says what is true now rather than what used to be.

No extension the reader knows, no highlighter language, no headings — so the Contents
rail stays away and the page is monospace, wrapped in nothing.

Tabs	survive.
So do trailing spaces and a final newline.
